The Best Warframes for Beginners

Early in Warframe, some Warframes make the journey much easier: easy survival, reliable damage and fast farming. Here are our beginner picks.

Rhino — the unkillable tank

Rhino is THE beginner pick: Iron Skin makes him nearly invincible for a while, and Rhino Charge deals big damage. Easy to get (Jackal boss on Venus). His Prime set is also very accessible — see the Rhino Prime price.

Excalibur — versatile and solid

Available early for many players, Excalibur excels in melee with his Exalted Blade and stays useful at all levels.

Frost / Volt — control and damage

Volt brings speed and electric damage (great early/mid game); Frost protects with his Snow Globe (ideal for Defense missions).

How to get more Warframes

Most Warframes are farmed by beating bosses (their components) then crafting them. You can also buy Prime parts from other players: compare prices on the parts & blueprints and Prime sets pages.
