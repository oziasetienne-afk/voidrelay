How does the rotation work?

The Circuit is Duviri's endless roguelite mode. Each week it offers a fixed choice: 3 Warframes (and their blueprints) in the normal Circuit, and 5 Incarnon Genesis adapters in the Steel Path difficulty Circuit. Before launching, you pick your target reward, then climb the tiers by chaining rounds — the adapter drops at the matching tier. The choice stays identical all week, then rotates on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

How to pick your weapon

  • Prioritise what you already own: the adapter applies to the base weapon — if you no longer have it, you'll need to rebuild it (or buy it back, depending on the weapon) before enjoying it.
  • Adapters cannot be traded: you can't buy a weapon's Incarnon form on the market — only the Circuit rotation grants it. If a weapon interests you this week, it's now or in several weeks.
  • Think end-game: the Incarnon form often turns an average weapon into a Steel Path-viable one — check our builds to mod what you unlock.
  • Favourite pick: if the Dual Toxocyst show up during the week, take their adapter — Pandaahh rates them the best secondary in the game: the Incarnon form's ricochets score continuous headshots, so the passive sustains itself.

Getting ready

The Steel Path Circuit demands solid equipment: properly ranked mods, arcanes, and a Warframe that survives. If you're starting end-game, read where to start the Steel Path and check your mods' prices on the item pages. The live world state (cycles, fissures, Baro) is on the dashboard.

The typical run, timed live (Pandaahh, 10 tiers in one go): expect 2h to 2h30 fully focused for all 10 tiers at once — long, but doable in an evening, and nothing forces you to chain it all: progress is kept during the week. His squad tips: bring something to handle Eximus Overguard (Magnetic — +100% damage from the first stack — or the companion mod Assassin Posture), always keep one excavation cell in reserve to restart an extractor that loses its shield, and stay alert on defense, the real hot moment of the run. Decrees do the rest: stacked well, even level 10,000 enemies drop in one shot. The earned adapter is then installed at Cavalero's, on the Zariman.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Incarnon rotation change?

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC. The weapons shown here stay valid all week.

How do I get an Incarnon Genesis adapter?

Launch the Steel Path Circuit from Duviri, pick one of the week's 5 weapons as your target reward, then climb the tiers up to the adapter.

How long do the 10 Circuit tiers take?

About 2h to 2h30 in one sitting while staying focused (timed live by Pandaahh). No need to do it all at once: tier progress is kept all week.

Do I need the Steel Path?

Yes for Incarnons: only Steel Path difficulty Circuits offer them. The normal Circuit gives Warframes and their blueprints.
