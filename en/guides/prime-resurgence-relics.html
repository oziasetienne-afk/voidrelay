Prime Resurgence and relics: how to get Primes

Want a vaulted Prime that no longer appears in normal drops? Two routes: Prime Resurgence and the market.

Prime Resurgence (Varzia)

Varzia, at Maroo's Bazaar, offers a rotation of vaulted Prime Warframes and weapons for Aya and Regal Aya. It is the official way to get an old Prime without going through the market. The rotation has no predefined order: the next resurgence is only revealed mid-cycle, about two weeks before the end. The current rotation and location are shown live on the dashboard.

Void relics

Relics (Lith, Meso, Neo, Axi) contain the Prime parts. To open them:

  1. Equip a relic matching the part you want.
  2. Run a Void Fissure mission of the right era.
  3. Collect 10 Void Reactant during the mission to "crack" your relic.
  4. At the end, pick among the rewards (in a squad: 4 choices).

Refine your relics (Void Traces) up to Radiant to maximise your rare-part chances — our relic calculator gives you the exact odds, and Relics tonight tells you which relics to open right now.

Buying directly

Don't feel like farming? You can buy the parts or the full set from other players. Always compare parts vs set prices on the item page before buying.
