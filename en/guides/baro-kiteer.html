Baro Ki'Teer: Schedule and What to Buy

Baro Ki'Teer, the Void Trader, is a travelling merchant who sells exclusive items for ducats and credits.

When does Baro Ki'Teer return?

Baro arrives every 14 days and stays 48 hours in a Relay, then leaves with his stock. You can see his next visit and live inventory on the VoidRelay dashboard (countdown included).

What to buy first

  • Primed mods (Primed Continuity, Primed Flow, etc.): the best long-term buys — some later resell for a lot of platinum.
  • Exclusive weapons and cosmetics, depending on taste and resale.
  • Avoid spending ducats on decorations if you're optimizing.

How to prepare

Before he arrives, stock up on ducats by recycling common Prime parts. Aim for 2,000–3,000 ducats to grab several Primed mods.

Resale tip: some Primed mods bought from Baro resell for lots of platinum later. Watch the deals.
