Arbitrations: farm Vitus Essence efficiently (and turn it into platinum)

Arbitrations are the end-game elite alerts: one life only, buffed enemies… and the only source of Vitus Essence, the Arbiters of Hexis' currency. Done well, a session is truly worth it: this method — tested live by Pandaahh — produced 700 Vitus in 1h27, with 3 million credits and thousands of endo along the way.

Prerequisite: the whole star chart completed Best mission: Interception Proven yield: 700 Vitus / 1h27

The principle: drones, boosters, long phases

  • Vitus drops from Arbitration drones: ~6% chance per drone, 12% with a resource booster — and even more with a relay's resource blessing. Stack both before every session.
  • ⚠️ After taking a blessing, switch relay (or go back through the Orbiter) before launching the mission, otherwise it isn't active. Squad tip: switching relay avoids reforming the squad.
  • Pick up Vitus immediately — the days of leaving it on the ground are over.
  • Choose Interception when it's on rotation: no NPCs to protect, and by keeping a ~1% lead over the enemy you stretch each phase — hence more drones, more Vitus. Hold the points two per point and "pre-heat" the spawn zones.

The livestream loadout

💥 Kuva Ogris + Nightwatch NapalmThe Nightwatch Napalm mod (drops from Kela De Thaym, Sedna — and by repeating her you also farm the Sobek mod that resells for 30-50 pl) leaves fire pools: shoot every spawn zone and everything dies continuously. With the right anti-faction mod, base damage, multishot, Firestorm (bigger area) and ammo mutation. Details on the Kuva Ogris page.
🧪 Saryn (or another buffer)Viral element on the weapon (a simple Toxin mod: her kit already adds poison and corrosive, and her 1's augment gives the team Corrosive). Mirage, Volt, Rhino, Chroma or Jade also work — and a full-strength Wisp for the motes (HP + speed, not the shock mote: you want enemies to keep walking in).
🔋 Energized Munitions (Helminth)The Helminth ability itself (75% ammo efficiency ≈ 4 shots for 1) solves the Ogris' real problem. Complete with Protea specters (ammo dispensers) or Nidus ones (strength buff), and Akarius as secondary.
🐾 Companion with doubled pickupsAdarza kavat (crit) or Sahasa kubrow (utility), with the mod that doubles pickups (Loyal Retriever) — essential here.

💡 Anti-knockdown trick: caught without protection? Deploy your Archgun: it grants ~2,000 Overguard — which immunises against knockdowns — and half a second of invulnerability when it breaks. Enough to survive any deployment.

What to spend Vitus on

  • Reselling for platinum — at the Arbiters of Hexis (any relay): Galvanized mods, Preparation, Rolling Guard, Adaptation… in high demand from the wave of new players. That's the real "Vitus → platinum" engine — compare prices on our item pages.
  • Kuva — in packs at the Arbiters: a long session (~2h) represents on the order of 400,000 Kuva to roll your Rivens.
  • Arcanes, aura forma, cosmetics — as needs arise. Arcanes also drop directly in the mission.

The session's real loot (1h27, squad of 4)

  • 700 Vitus Essence (240 within the first 28 minutes)
  • 3,000,000 credits · 7,000 endo + Ayatan sculptures (Defense remains better for endo: ~40,000 in 40 min)
  • 110 Orokin Cells · 198,000 alloy plates · 260 Riven Slivers · 2 arcanes · dozens of mods

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock Arbitrations?

Complete every mission on every planet. Arbitrations then rotate in the alerts: one life only, buffed enemies — aim for a solid build before settling in (see starting the end-game right).

What drops Vitus?

The Arbitration drones: ~6% chance per drone, doubled by a resource booster, increased further by a blessing. Pick it up right away.

Why Interception rather than Defense?

No NPCs to protect, and phases you can stretch to multiply drones. Defense remains the best for endo.

How much is Vitus worth in platinum?

A lot: the Arbiters' Galvanized mods, Preparation, Rolling Guard and Adaptation resell very well — check current prices on the item pages before choosing what to buy.
