Coda weapons: how to get them

Coda weapons are NOT a Lich system: no hunting, no Requiem mods to guess. They're bought directly from Eleanor, a merchant of The Hex syndicate in Höllvania, using Live Heartcell.

How to get them

  1. Defeat a Technocyte Coda (an Infested enemy found in Höllvania): it drops Live Heartcell.
  2. Visit Eleanor (The Hex, Höllvania Central Mall): each Coda weapon costs 10 Live Heartcell.
  3. No relics, no mods to hunt for — the price is paid entirely in Heartcell.

The Progenitor bonus

Every Coda weapon carries a random elemental damage bonus (the "Progenitor bonus"), assigned at the moment of purchase. Don't like the roll? No need to farm from scratch — Eleanor's offer refreshes every 4 days, with a fresh roll each time.

The 14 Coda weapons

Bassocyst (shotgun), Bubonico (rifle), Catabolyst (pistol), Caustacyst (melee), Hema (rifle), Hirudo (melee), Mire (melee), Motovore (melee), Pathocyst (melee), Pox (pistol), Sporothrix (sniper), Synapse (rifle), Tysis (pistol) and Dual Coda Torxica (pistol). All introduced with Update 42.0 ("The Shadowgrapher").

For Warframe's other random-reward systems, see our Kuva Lich guide (Kuva weapons, Requiem mods) — a different mechanic, don't mix them up.
