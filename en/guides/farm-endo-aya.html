Farming Endo and Aya efficiently

Endo ranks up your mods, and Aya buys vaulted Prime Resurgence items from Varzia. Two end-game currencies, two very different ways to farm them — here are the best.

Farming Endo

  • Arbitrations: the best passive yield — every rotation has a chance to drop a solid chunk of Endo, on any Arbitration mission.
  • Ayatan sculptures: pick them up in missions (especially Spy and caves), slot Ayatan stars into them, then dissolve them at Cephalon Simaris in a Relay — often several thousand Endo at once for a well-filled sculpture.
  • Void relics: a small amount of Endo shows up regularly among Fissure rewards, alongside Prime parts.
  • Sell your spare mods: from the mod management screen, convert duplicates and junk mods into Endo — low yield, but free.

Nekros and his Desecrate boost your sculpture and relic drop chances on the mission — useful if you chain Endo farms in a squad.

Farming Aya

Netracells (missions exclusive to the Steel Path) are the main Aya source, with a guaranteed bonus on the first Netracell completed each day — run them daily for a steady flow. Sanctuary Onslaught (regular or Elite) also drops some, in more modest amounts, if you prefer an endless mission over a Netracell.

Once your stock is built up, spend your Aya at Varzia during a Prime Resurgence window — see our dedicated guide for what to grab first.
