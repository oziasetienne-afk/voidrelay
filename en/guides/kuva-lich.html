Kuva Lich: weapon, element, and how to get rid of it

A Kuva Lich spawns when you damage a Larvling (a Grineer enemy that transforms mid-mission) without finishing it off: it flees and becomes your personal nemesis, with a random Kuva weapon and element. Good news: you don't need to guess its weakness blind — and you can even steer the roll from the start.

Choosing the element at the Lich's birth

It's not entirely random: the weapon's default element (and the ephemera's theme, if you kill it) depends on the Warframe equipped for the mercy kill on the Larvling. Each Warframe is tied to one of 7 elements:

  • Impact: Baruuk, Dante, Gauss, Grendel, Rhino, Sevagoth, Sirius & Orion, Wukong, Zephyr.
  • Heat: Chroma, Ember, Inaros, Jade, Kullervo, Nezha, Protea, Temple, Uriel, Vauban, Wisp.
  • Cold: Frost, Gara, Hildryn, Koumei, Revenant, Styanax, Titania, Trinity.
  • Electricity: Banshee, Caliban, Excalibur, Follie, Gyre, Limbo, Nova, Valkyr, Volt.
  • Toxin: Atlas, Dagath, Ivara, Khora, Nekros, Nidus, Nokko, Oberon, Oraxia, Saryn.
  • Magnetic: Citrine, Cyte-09, Harrow, Hydroid, Lavos, Mag, Mesa, Xaku, Yareli.
  • Radiation: Ash, Equinox, Garuda, Loki, Mirage, Nyx, Octavia, Qorvex, Voruna.

A Warframe's Primed or Umbra version keeps the same element as its base version. Table verified 2026-07-10 against the official Warframe wiki's data module (wiki.warframe.com), cross-checked with an independent second guide: 2 recent entries (Sirius & Orion, Follie) only appear in one of the two sources, most likely because the third-party guide hasn't been updated since their release yet.

Finding the element and mods to avoid

Every Kuva Siphon or Kuva Flood mission has you kill your Lich's Thralls: each one reveals a clue that rules out one candidate Requiem mod. After enough clues, your Vanquish card directly shows the Lich's element and which mods NOT to use — you just need to find the right order of the remaining 3 mods by elimination during the final showdown.

The Requiem mods

There are 8 Requiem mods (Netra, Ris, Vome, Khra, Xata, Jahu, Oull, Lohk), obtained by opening Requiem relics. Each Lich is vulnerable to exactly 3 of them, in a specific order, equipped on your Parazon. Oull is the exception: it's a wildcard that replaces any of the 8 symbols — handy if your collection is incomplete.

During the fight: avoid its element

Your Lich's weapon carries one of the classic damage elements. Using that same element on it heals it instead of hurting it — check the element shown on its card and adjust your weapon (or temporarily remove the matching elemental mod) before the confrontation.

Kill or convert?

  • Killing gets you the weapon immediately, with a chance at the Lich's ephemera (a cosmetic effect unique to each theme).
  • Converting turns the Lich into an ally who joins you on request in missions. No ephemera, and the weapon isn't usable right away.

Get the mod combo wrong and the Lich flees with extra health/damage (and permanently rules out one Requiem symbol for itself) — no big deal, just run another Siphon/Flood mission to keep narrowing it down.

What about Sisters of Parvos?

The Sisters of Parvos system (Corpus) works identically, but grants Tenet weapons instead of Kuva ones. Everything above (clues, Requiem mods, kill/convert) applies the same way. Don't confuse this with Coda weapons: despite the similar-sounding searches, it's NOT a Lich system — they're bought directly from a merchant, no hunting involved.
